About

One person, responsible for the work.

Vaccaro Construction Services is a locally owned, owner-operated construction and home improvement business specializing in bathroom remodeling, general home repairs, and small renovations.

With years of hands-on experience working alongside established contracting companies, I bring professional knowledge, quality workmanship, and personal attention to every project.

I work directly with homeowners and property managers to provide dependable solutions for a wide range of construction, remodeling, and home maintenance needs. Whether you're updating a bathroom, tackling a list of repairs, or making improvements to your property, Vaccaro Construction Services is committed to getting the job done right.
